Calculation

Mun,lin(j): Linear unloading constrained compression modulus
Mun,lin2(j): Second unloading constrained compression modulus
Mun,sec(j): Secant unloading constrained compression modulus
Mre,lin1(j): First linear reloading constrained compression modulus
Mre,lin2(j): Second linear reloading constrained compression modulus
Mi: Initial constrained compression modulus
Mf: Final constrained compression modulus
